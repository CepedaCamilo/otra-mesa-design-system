# Yaku Design System

The living catalogue of **Yaku**, table ordering for a Peruvian–Nikkei restaurant in Hamburg: guests scan the QR on their table,
browse the menu and send their order straight to the kitchen. Every component here is the real one the app uses.

**How it is built**

- **Shared components, Yaku’s skin.** The buttons, tabs, filters, fields and the language switch are the same React + TypeScript
  components as in the [Otra Mesa design system](https://storybook.danies.trade). Behaviour, keyboard and accessibility are written and
  tested once; everything you see — canvas texture, *ají amarillo*, Jost and Td Neumann — comes from Yaku’s own tokens.
- **Two moods.** Light, on the restaurant’s canvas, and a warm black for the evening. Use the theme switch in the toolbar.
- **Checked automatically.** Every story runs an accessibility check (axe) in both modes; see the **Accessibility** panel.
