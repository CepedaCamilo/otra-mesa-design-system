# Colour & type

The restaurant’s canvas by day and a warm black by night — never a green or blue tint, so the food photos keep their colour.
**One accent, ají amarillo** (the Peruvian chili): it always marks the next step. Every colour has a light and a dark value,
read straight from `marcas/yaku.css`.

<Paleta />

## Contrast, calculated

Every text-on-background pair the app really uses, measured with the WCAG 2.2 formula. Body text needs 4.5:1.

<Contraste />

## Type

Td Neumann for dish names and titles, in capitals — the voice of the restaurant’s menu. Jost for everything you read and tap.
