# 0005 · Customer app always light; business tools with a dark mode

**Status:** accepted · 13 Oct 2026 (design system 1.5.0, "Noche verde" dark mode)

**Context.** The customer app is used briefly, often by day and outdoors. The business portal and the back office stay open for
hours, also at night and in dim kitchens.

**Decision.** The customer app is always light, whatever the device setting. The business tools get a dark mode that follows the
device until the person picks one; every colour has a dark value with checked contrast.

**Why.** The customer app is a shop window — light and food. The brand is clean and cheerful, and dishes and brand colours shine
on white. The business tools are work tools used for hours and at night, where dark is easier on the eyes.

**Trade-offs.** Some customers prefer dark mode and the app does not follow it — a conscious choice to put the brand first in
the shop window. Two behaviours in one product need explaining to the team.

**What would change it.** If data showed most customers want it dark, I would design a dark mode made for food — not inverted
colours — and test it with the real photos.
