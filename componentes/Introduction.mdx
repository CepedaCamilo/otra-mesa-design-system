# Otra Mesa Design System

The living catalogue of the components behind **Otra Mesa**, a food-rescue platform for Barranquilla with three apps:
the customer app, the business portal and the back office. Every component here is the real one the apps use.

**How it is built**

- **Tokens first.** Colour, type, space, radius, shadow and height come from one `tokens.css`, with a light and a dark value for every colour.
- **One component, three apps.** A change here changes every app at once — no copies drifting apart.
- **TypeScript as a guard rail.** The component types make wrong usages impossible, including accessibility rules
  (an icon-only button cannot exist without an accessible name).
- **Checked in light and dark.** Use the theme switch in the toolbar. Every story runs an automatic accessibility check (axe);
  see the **Accessibility** panel.
