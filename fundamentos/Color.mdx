# Colour

Clean and white, with **one text colour** — a near-black with a hint of green. The logo green carries primary actions and active
states; the cheerful terracotta is kept for **small details only** (stars, underlines, map dots). Every colour has a light and a dark
value. Dark mode ("Noche verde") is used by the business portal and the back office; the customer app is always light.

<Paleta grupo="Color" />

## Contrast, calculated

Every text-on-background pair the apps really use, measured with the WCAG 2.2 formula from the values in `tokens.css`.
Body text needs 4.5:1; icons and large text need 3:1.
