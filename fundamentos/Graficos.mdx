# Charts · palette "Caribe"

Six categorical colours in a **fixed order**. The order is what keeps neighbours apart for colour-blind readers, so series always take
slots 1, 2, 3… and never skip or reorder. Validated with a protanopia/deuteranopia simulation: the closest neighbouring pair stays
at ΔE 18.3 in light and 14.7 in dark (minimum 8). Maps and scatter plots use at most three colours; a seventh category folds into "Other".

The rows below simulate how each colour-vision type sees the palette (Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes, 2009).
