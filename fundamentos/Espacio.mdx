# Space & shape

Every measurement comes from a token. The scales replaced 27 loose spacing values, 12 radii and 14 shadows.

## Space and control heights

Fixed steps from 2 to 48 px. Buttons and inputs have the same height in all three apps.

<Espacios />

## Radius

Five corner radii, from rows and QR codes to bottom sheets and pills.

<Radios />

## Shadow

Four shadows, always tinted with the text green. In dark mode shadows disappear, so cards are separated by a fine line instead.
